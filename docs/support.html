Getting started

Blocking isn't working

Zen-ly needs Screen Time access to block anything. If you skipped it during setup, the Focus screen shows a "Screen Time access needed" card and Begin focus stays dimmed. Tap Grant Access there, or go to Settings → Screen Time → Grant Screen Time Access. You'll be asked to confirm with Face ID or your passcode — that's iOS, not Zen-ly.

Choosing what gets blocked

Open Settings → Focus profiles and pick a profile, or add one from Schedule → New profile. Under Blocking you can:

  • Block all apps — everything except Phone, Messages, Settings and Zen-ly.
  • Allowed apps — exceptions that stay open while everything else is blocked.
  • Research mode — list specific websites and Safari is limited to only those during focus. Useful for working without wandering.

Why can't Zen-ly show the names of the apps I blocked?

It genuinely can't see them. Apple's Screen Time framework hands apps an opaque token instead of a name or identifier, so your selections stay private even from Zen-ly. That's a deliberate part of how iOS protects this data — see the privacy policy.

Sessions

Ending a session early

Tap End early on the session screen. If the profile has Strict mode on, there's a deliberate five-second wait before the confirm button becomes tappable — the friction is the point. Sessions ended early don't count toward your streak or your daily minutes.

My session disappeared when iOS closed the app

It didn't. Zen-ly stores the session outside the app, so if iOS shuts it down mid-focus the timer keeps its real end time. Reopen the app and it either resumes with the correct time remaining, or — if the session finished while you were away — records it against the day it actually started.

Taking a break

Give a profile a non-zero break length and you'll be offered Take a break when a focus session completes. Breaks don't block anything.

Schedules

My schedule didn't start on its own

iOS doesn't let an app launch itself in the background, so a scheduled session begins when Zen-ly is open — or when you tap the notification it sends at the start time. Make sure the schedule is toggled on, that today is one of its selected weekdays, and that Screen Time access has been granted.

How long is a scheduled session?

It runs to the end of the window you set, so an 8-hour schedule blocks for the remainder of those 8 hours — not the profile's usual session length.

Widget, Lock Screen and Siri

Add the Zen-ly widget from the Home Screen and choose which stat it shows. While a session runs you'll get a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. You can also start a session with "Hey Siri, start a focus session in Zen-ly", from the Shortcuts app, or via the Control Center control on iOS 18 and later.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. Zen-ly has no account, no server and no analytics, so there is nothing to sign into and nothing to recover remotely. Deleting the app deletes your history with it. Full detail is in the privacy policy.
